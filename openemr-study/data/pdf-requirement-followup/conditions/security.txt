

<proposed-pdf-export-security-requirement>
The Eye Exam PDF shall include the clinical narrative and authorized report
images. User-controlled narrative shall be rendered as content: HTML, CSS,
URL, or file-path syntax within it shall not be interpreted as a resource
reference by the PDF generator. The generator may load images and other
resources only through application-controlled references to authorized
clinical media or approved report assets.
</proposed-pdf-export-security-requirement>
